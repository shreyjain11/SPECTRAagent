  `2026-11-02.1`; finish-review version `2026-10-29.1` and its text are unchanged.
  This revision changes service access and sharing, not the scientific evidence
  or completion criteria used to interpret an earlier audit.

  sources. Registry data and the submission service remain hosted separately;
  submitting an audit does not automatically publish it.
